# Lexávelo
![Coat of arms of the project](./Lexavelo_logo1.jpg)

## Introduction
Lexávelo is a literal modification of English created by Classicuscool. His intent was to grow a community around this project. However, after his account was hacked, he created a new server around this project, but renamed "Antarlang".

The creator is a very young Asian student and gamer who tries to speak English and Spanish.

You can find the official wiki [here](https://lexavelo-official.fandom.com/wiki/Lexavelo_Official_Wiki).

## Alphabet
Lexavelo uses the full Latin alphabet, which Classicus pronounces as Spanish. A couple of words have acute accents for varied reasons, such as making the name of the project unique.

## Grammar
The creator uses his understanding of English grammar plus some conscious choices, yielding the following differences:

- No word compounding
- Almost no inflection
- Postpositive adjectives
- No plurals
- No genders
- Few tenses from English

## Vocabulary
Classicus adds his favorite words to the vocabulary either by looking at word maps of Europe or experimenting with Google Translate. The only consistent aspect of the vocabulary is the ending of words.
