Veluil

The Versatile Efficient Logical Unambiguous International Language (veluil) is an unique conlang being created by hihhih from South Korea since 2023. It prioritizes maximum compactness, objectivity and carbon neutrality. As such, it shares traits with programming languages, such as functions and the hexadecimal system. Also, images are avoided.
