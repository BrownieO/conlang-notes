Grammar

Verbs

Verbs act as programming functions. The first parameter is an object or subject complement. The last or only parameter is the subject. E.g.

  • mımtti memppu jjo
  • be human me
  • I am a human

Verb "to be"

All messages get the verb "to be" implicitly at the start. You only need to add a pronoun at the end.

E.g. memppu jjo = I am a human; memppu jju = this is a human.

Nesting

Functions are nestable. Consider this explicit function:

  • la memppu jjo
  • ask human me
  • Asks me about a human

The full message nests that function inside the implicit "to be" one. I.e.

  • (mımtti) la memppu jjo jju
  • (be) ask human me this
  • This asks me about a human

Here's another example:

  • (mımtti) la me bımppu di jjo
  • (be) ask of name you me
  • I'm asking your name
