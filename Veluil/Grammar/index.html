Grammar

Verb "to be"

A lone noun gets the verb "to be" on its right side. There are two default implicit pronouns for ths verb: "I" for speech and "this" for text.

E.g. memppu jjo = I am human; memppu = this is a human.

Verbs

Verbs act as programming functions, accepting a subject and sometimes an object. This yields a Verb-Object-Subject order.

E.g. la memppu jjo <=> ask human me <=> "Thing that asks me about a human".

Functions are nestable.

E.g. la me bımppu di <=> ask of name you <=> "What is your name?".
